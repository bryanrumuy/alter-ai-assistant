# Kopi Senja (fictional cafe used for demos and tests)

## Opening hours
- Open every day from 8:00 am to 10:00 pm (Western Indonesia Time, WIB).
- Closed on the first day of Eid al-Fitr.

## Menu and prices (Indonesian rupiah)
- Iced latte: Rp28,000
- Americano: Rp22,000
- Matcha latte: Rp30,000
- Butter croissant: Rp24,000
- Cheesecake slice: Rp32,000
- Plant-based milk (oat) is available for any drink at no extra charge.
- Our menu contains dairy, gluten, eggs and nuts in some items. For allergy questions, ask the team.

## How to order
- Pickup only: order here in the chat and collect at the counter. We do not deliver.
- Payment at the counter: cash or QRIS.

## Policies
- A pickup order can be cancelled up to 10 minutes after it is placed.
- Refunds and complaints are handled by the manager, not by this assistant.
- Large or catering orders (more than 20 items) and discounts are agreed with the manager.

## Emergencies
- For medical or safety emergencies customers should call 112 (Indonesia) first; the team will follow up.

## Location
- Jl. Contoh No. 1, Surabaya (demo address).
